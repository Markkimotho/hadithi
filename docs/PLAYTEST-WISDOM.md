# Wisdom world playtest

This is the human gate for the third-edition vertical slice. Run `npm run build`,
then `PORT=4174 npm start` and open `http://127.0.0.1:4174`. Start a fresh
Wisdom save in World view. Note the browser/device, screen size, and motion
setting with each finding.

## Desktop

- Move with WASD and arrows; inspect the covered tracks; pick up the cord; walk
  to the market and well. Confirm walking costs no turn and the well stays on
  the shared market ground.
- Follow the forest route to the river and ford. Try the stones once, then try
  a safer route after gathering evidence. Check that water pushes loose props
  and that a failed skill still moves the story forward.
- Reach Anansi's tree and try the balance challenge both before and after
  teaching him the carrying knot. Compare the weight position and difficulty.
- Reach the evening circle with different evidence. Confirm the scale only
  offers the clues you found, and that taking too long has a clear consequence.
- Enter the squall. Check rain, dusk light, gust movement, and the brace
  challenge. Switch to Classic view and confirm the story is still playable.
- Reload and undo at a safe point. Confirm story actions and skill outcomes
  replay consistently.

## Phone

- Repeat movement and travel using tap-to-move and the joystick. Check that
  hotspots, prompts, controls, and challenge buttons fit in portrait and
  landscape without blocking the route.
- Try a timing challenge and the scale challenge by touch. Confirm the player
  can cancel and continue without a turn being spent.
- Enable reduced motion and repeat the challenges. Confirm environmental
  particles settle to a still frame and timing can be advanced by button.

## Record

For each issue, include the steps, expected result, actual result, and a
screenshot or short clip. Mark unclear dialogue, unfair challenge timing,
blocked paths, confusing map markers, and anything that makes the tale feel
less like a Fante storyteller's telling. Review cultural phrasing with an
Akan reader before release.
