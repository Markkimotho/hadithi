# Sources and credits

Hadithi's four tales are new, playable retellings of traditional stories. The scenes, characters' words, puzzles, endings and art were made for Hadithi. No source text or illustrations are reproduced. Every tradition has many tellings; these are retellings of one published version each, and the game's own choices (the trials, the extra endings, the secrets) are ours, not the tradition's.

| Tale | Tradition | Retold from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Wisdom in the Pot | Fante, Ghana | "How Wisdom Became the Property of the Human Race", in W. H. Barker and Cecilia Sinclair, *West African Folk-Tales* (1917). [Read it](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/66923) |
| The Palace Beneath the Tide | Japanese, Tango coast | "The Story of Urashima Taro, the Fisher Lad", in Yei Theodora Ozaki, *Japanese Fairy Tales* (1903). [Read it](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4018) |
| The Midnight Orchestra | German, Grimm collection | "The Travelling Musicians", Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm, in the English edition after Edgar Taylor and Marian Edwardes. [Read it](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2591) |
| The Olive Jar | Baghdad, *The Thousand and One Nights* | "Story of Ali Cogia, Merchant of Bagdad", in Andrew Lang (ed.), *The Arabian Nights Entertainments* (1898), from Antoine Galland's French. [Read it](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/128) |

The editions consulted are in the public domain in the United States. Each tale's **Source** button in the game shows the same notes.

## What we are still checking

- A reader from each tradition is reviewing how the tales are framed, in particular the Akan storytelling opener and the Baghdad setting.
- Rights for each source edition outside the United States.

If you know a tradition well and something reads wrong, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Markkimotho/hadithi/issues).

## Made with

- Everything you see in the world (walls, ground, skies, people, animals and things) is painted by the game's own code. There are no image files.
- Sound is made live as you play. There are no recordings.
- Typefaces, all under the SIL Open Font License: Pixelify Sans (titles and labels), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (reading text), Literata and Fraunces.
- Physics by [Planck.js](https://piqnt.com/planck.js/), a JavaScript port of Box2D (MIT).
- The first-person renderer follows the approach of classic raycasting games.
