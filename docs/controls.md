# Controls

## Keyboard and mouse

| Do this | With |
| --- | --- |
| Walk forward and back | **W** **S**, or **↑** **↓** |
| Step sideways | **A** **D** |
| Turn | **←** **→**, or the mouse |
| Run | hold **Shift** |
| Act on what the crosshair is on, or go on with the story | **E** or **Space** |
| Choose a line in a conversation | **1** to **9** |
| Lift or set down a loose thing | **Q** |
| Throw what you carry | **F** |
| Fold the story to one line, or open it again | **H** |
| Open the journal | **J** (or **B** bag, **K** what you know, **P** people, **M** map) |
| Take back your last step | **U** |
| Stop walking, or let go of the mouse | **Esc** |
| The in-game guide | **?** |

Click the view once to let the mouse turn you freely. Press **Esc** to get the pointer back. You can also drag across the view to look around without capturing the mouse.

Click a signpost to walk down that road. Click a person or a glinting thing in the view to walk up to it.

## Phone and tablet

- **The stick** (bottom left) walks you forward and back and turns you.
- **Swipe** anywhere on the view to look around.
- **Tap** a person or a glinting thing to walk to it and act. Tap a signpost to take that road.
- **E** (bottom right) acts and moves the story on. **Q** and **F** appear when you can lift or throw something.
- Tap the story panel to go on. Tap **Fold** to shrink it to one line.

## Controller

The left stick walks and steps sideways, the right stick turns. **A** acts and moves the story on, **X** lifts or sets down, **Y** throws, and holding **B** runs. The D-pad walks and turns too.
