# Troubleshooting

**The world doesn't open, or the screen stays on "The storyteller clears their throat".**
Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Strict content blocking or some privacy extensions can stop the game's scripts from loading; allow the site and reload.

**It runs slowly.**
The game lowers its own drawing resolution when a device struggles. Closing other tabs helps. Turning off **Scene motion** in Settings removes camera sway and weather particles.

**The mouse won't turn me.**
Click the view once so the game can hold the mouse. Press **Esc** to let go. Or drag across the view to look around.

**The story panel is in my way.**
Press **H** (or tap **Fold**) to shrink it to one line. When nothing is being told, it steps away by itself.

**Something is in the way and I can't walk there.**
Loose things like baskets and stools can block a path. Push them aside, or lift one with **Q** and set it down elsewhere.

**I can't do something I can see.**
Its diamond is grey. Walk up to it: the prompt says what you still need, often something to find or a connection to make in your journal.

**My progress is gone.**
Saves are kept in the browser you played in. A private window, a different browser, or clearing site data starts fresh. Use **Export save** in the journal to keep a copy.

**"A different edition."**
A tale was updated in a way your old journey can't follow. Start a new journey; your endings, echoes and marks are kept.
