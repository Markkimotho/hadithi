# Getting started

## Open the game

Go to **[hadithi-i2us.onrender.com](https://hadithi-i2us.onrender.com)** in a current browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer, phone or tablet. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

After the first visit the game works offline. You can add it to your home screen on a phone and play it like an app.

## Pick a tale

The title screen shows the four tales. The picture behind them is the opening place of whichever tale you are looking at. Use the arrow keys or your mouse to move between them, then:

- **Play** starts a new telling.
- **Continue** picks up where you left off.
- **Options** (or **Start over**) lets you begin again, choose whether to carry echoes from earlier tellings, make trials easier, or replay a particular seed.
- **Source** tells you where the tale comes from.

## Your first minutes

1. The storyteller opens the tale. Read at your own pace: press **E** or **Space** (or tap the panel) to go on.
2. A card names the place you are in. Look around: move the mouse, or press the arrow keys.
3. Gold diamonds float over things you can look at, take or talk to. Walk up to one with **W**, put the crosshair on it, and press **E**.
4. Your objective is always in the top-left corner. The minimap in the top-right shows where you are and where the roads lead out.
5. Signposts on the screen name the roads out of a place. Walk off along a road to go there.

That is all you need. Everything else is in [how a telling works](how-it-plays.md).
