# Settings and accessibility

Open **Settings** from the title screen.

## Sound

- **Sound effects**: dice, discoveries, interruptions. Off until you turn it on.
- **Ambience**: rain, sea, market and night, made live as you play. There are no recordings.
- **Volume**.

Press **S** on the title screen to turn all sound on or off.

## Comfort

- **Physical trials**: on, you play leaps, climbs and arguments by hand. Off, seeded dice decide at the same odds. Either way, failing moves the story on.
- **Scene motion**: weather, camera sway, shake and screen effects. It starts off if your device asks for reduced motion.
- **Story text size**: standard, large or larger.
- **Storyteller's grace**: new journeys get +2 on every trial's odds. It is shown in every roll.
- **Carry echoes**: new journeys remember what earlier endings taught you.

## Reading

- The story, choices and journal are set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed to be easy to read. Nothing is smaller than 14 pixels.
- Fold the story to one line with **H** whenever you want more of the view, and open it again to read in full.
- Nothing in the story is timed. Read at your own pace; the world waits for you to press **E**.

## Other ways to play

- Every action can be reached without fine aiming: tap or click a person or thing to walk to it and act, or click a signpost to take that road.
- The journal and every dialog work by keyboard alone, and the game is checked against WCAG 2 A and AA with an automated audit.
- A screen reader hears the story as it is told, and every button is labelled.
- On a phone, the touch controls step aside while a conversation or trial is open.
