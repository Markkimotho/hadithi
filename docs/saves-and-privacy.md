# Saves and privacy

## Your journey saves itself

Hadithi saves after every step, in your browser on this device. Close the tab and come back later: **Continue** picks up where you were.

Endings found, echoes learned, marks earned and your recent tellings are kept in the chronicle, even after you start a tale again.

## Moving to another device

1. Open the journal (**J**) and choose **Export save**. A small file downloads.
2. On the other device, open **Settings** on the title screen and choose **Import a journey**.

## Seeds

Every telling has a seed, up to seven letters and numbers, shown in your chronicle. Enter a seed under **Options** on a tale's card to replay the same luck. A link with `?seed=CODE` on the end starts new tellings with that seed.

## Privacy

- No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your saves stay in your browser. Nothing about your play is sent anywhere.
- Clearing your browser's site data for Hadithi deletes your saves. Export a save first if you want to keep it.
