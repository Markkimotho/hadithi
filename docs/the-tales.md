# The tales

No spoilers past the first scene.

## The Wisdom in the Pot

*Fante, present-day Ghana. A trickster adventure.*

Anansi is carrying the world's wisdom into the forest in a great clay pot. You are Kweku Tsin, his son, and the one person who saw him leave. Follow him through the market and the forest, read a river, cross it, and climb after a father who does not want to be followed.

- You play: Kweku Tsin
- Length: 15 to 25 minutes
- Content notes: family conflict, storms, falls. No graphic violence.

## The Palace Beneath the Tide

*Japan, the Tango coast. A time-slip mystery.*

You are Urashima Tarō, a young fisherman. At low tide you find a sea turtle tangled in old fishing line. Free it, and it offers you a journey to a palace under the sea, where all four seasons share one wall. Time runs differently there. Watch the tide.

- You play: Urashima Tarō
- Length: 15 to 25 minutes
- Content notes: animal entanglement, separation, ageing, an uncertain homecoming.

## The Midnight Orchestra

*Germany, the Grimm collection. A woodland caper.*

You are a donkey nobody wants any more. On the road you gather a dog, a cat and a rooster in the same trouble, and they walk behind you as a band. Ahead is a house full of thieves and a plan that definitely needs a rehearsal.

- You play: the donkey
- Length: 15 to 25 minutes
- Content notes: threats against animals in the source premise, thieves, pursuit. No graphic violence.

## The Olive Jar

*Baghdad, The Thousand and One Nights. A courtyard mystery.*

A merchant's gold has vanished from a jar of olives, and the real judge has dismissed the case. Tonight, in a moonlit courtyard, a child playing at being judge will hear it again. That child is you.

- You play: the courtyard cadi, a child judge
- Length: 15 to 25 minutes
- Content notes: theft, a broken friendship, a bribe offered to a child. The source's death sentence is not shown.
